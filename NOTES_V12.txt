V12 = the real-map game ported from Godot 4 to Godot 3.6 with the GLES2 renderer (the same engine type
as your working APK), because many TVs do not support the Godot 4 renderers.
Changed for GLES2 / Godot 3:
- Arabic text removed (Godot 3 cannot draw Arabic). Names are English.
- Names float over the nearest places (14 at a time), because Godot 3 has no 3D text.
- Real shadows are OFF by default (use_shadows in main.gd) for speed. No glow, no sky shader.
- Sky = colour background + haze + sun/moon discs + clouds. Roads are one textured ribbon each.
- Wall window textures, asphalt/paving atlas, sand ground are loaded from data/*.dat (no import step).
- 24 cars, 30 pedestrians, 24 pilgrims for TV speed.
Kept: real OpenStreetMap roads/buildings/heights/names, Kaaba + Clock Tower, day/night, lamps,
traffic, police lights, changing room, minimap + full map, sounds (engine, horn, steps, crowd, birds).
NOT TESTED on a device. The build runs a 240-frame smoke test; send me any error text.
